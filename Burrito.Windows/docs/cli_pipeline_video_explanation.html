1. WebP Encoder (`cwebp.exe`)

Converts raster images to next-gen WebP format using Google's official WebP encoder in multi-threaded effort mode.

cwebp.exe -q -m 6 -mt -o
  • -q <quality> Target image quality factor (40 to 100, default 80).
  • -m 6 Maximum encoding effort level for optimal block search compression.
  • -mt Enables multi-threaded encoding for blazing CPU speed.
🎨
2. Dual-Stage PNG Optimization

A powerful 2-step pipeline: Stage 1 lossy quantization followed by Stage 2 lossless IDAT chunk optimization.

Stage 1: pngquant.exe --quality=- --speed 1 --strip --force Stage 2: oxipng.exe -o 4 --strip safe
  • pngMax = quality | pngMin = max(0, quality - 15)
  • --speed 1 Highest color palette quantization precision.
  • oxipng -o 4 Level 4 lossless optimization effort with safe metadata removal.
🎬
3. FFmpeg Video Transcoder & CRF Formula

Transcodes video into H.264 MP4 or VP9 WebM. Converts user 40-100 quality percentage into constant rate factor (CRF).

CRF = Math.round( 32 - ((videoQuality - 40) / 60.0) * 14 )
  • Quality 100 → CRF 18 (visually lossless).
  • Quality 40 → CRF 32 (maximum compression).
  • -movflags +faststart Moves MOOV atom to header for instant web playback.
